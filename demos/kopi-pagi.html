Our story

A family stall that never changed its recipe, only where you can find it.

1968

A stall in old-town Ipoh

Grandpa Lim roasts robusta with butter and sugar in a wok, pulls it through a cloth sock, and sells it from a pushcart.

1985

The first shop

Marble tables, green tiles and a queue out the door every morning. Kaya made fresh every dawn.

2012

The third generation joins

Same recipe, written down for the first time so it can travel.
